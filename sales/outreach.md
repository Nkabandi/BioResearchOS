the door. Three drafts below, plus the follow-up rule. Personalize the bracketed
bits (their lab, their paper, their programme) — that's the whole game.

## Rules

1. One message at a time. No sequence. Personalize the bracketed parts.
2. Always offer the free sample first. The sample IS the sale.
3. After 1 follow-up with no reply: move on. No stalking.
4. Log every contact in `leads.csv` (Status: contacted → replied → call → trial → client → testimonial).
