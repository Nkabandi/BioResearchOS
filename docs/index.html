How it works

2I reply with a fixed price and delivery date. No surprises.
3I deliver the review, analysis, or figures with a clear methods note and honest caveats.
4You review; one round of revisions included, then revise or done.
Important: I provide AI-assisted research support. You remain responsible for scientific interpretation and decisions, and you review the work before using it. Nothing here is medical or diagnostic advice.
