Standards

What every publication carries

These are not claims. They are the editorial rules of this desk.

Crossref verification

Every citation machine-checked against Crossref metadata before release.

DOIs, checked

References resolve. If a DOI fails, the source is dropped or replaced.

Evidence tables

Every finding links to a row, a paper, and a date — nothing floats.

Search protocol

Queries, databases, and inclusion criteria are recorded per volume.

PRISMA-inspired screening

Screened records and exclusions are counted and reported.

Limitations documented

Research gaps and constraints are stated explicitly — never concealed.

Reproducible workflow

From screening set to finding, every step can be rerun.

Public data only

No proprietary databases, no paywalled claims without a verified source.

"No hallucinations. Every claim traced to a source you can open."
