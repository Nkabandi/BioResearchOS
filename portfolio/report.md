## Key Findings

- Eight high-quality papers on AI-driven drug repurposing in rare diseases screened. Hybrid evidence–human machine learning, phenotype-based ML, and sex/KRAS-stratified models add value over pure ML. Prospective clinical validation is the field's central gap.


## Research Gaps

- No prospective randomized validation
- preprint-level evidence on ferroptosis
- n=1 case studies
- bench-to-clinic pipeline gap
