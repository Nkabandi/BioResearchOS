## Research Question

What is the current state of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) surveillance and bloodstream infection pathogen resistance in East Africa?

## Methods

- Targeted PubMed search of East African AMR surveillance and bloodstream infection studies

## Key Findings

- AMR-NAP implementation ranges 7% (Burundi) to 94% (Kenya)
- WHO surveillance checklist completion 44-100%. Resistance to first-line drugs is 50-100% (ampicillin, cotrimoxazole) with emerging ceftriaxone resistance 46-69% in Gram-negatives. First community-acquired ESBL bloodstream infections reported in Zanzibar
- MRSA 3.9-56.8% and ESBL 1.9-53% in healthcare-associated infections.


## Research Gaps

- Northern-southern representativeness of surveillance
- standardized regional AMR national reporting
- sustainable financing for national reference labs.
